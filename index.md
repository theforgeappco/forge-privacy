Privacy Policy
Effective date: May 2, 2026
This Privacy Policy describes how the Forge mobile application ("Forge", "the App") handles information about you. We've kept it short and plain.
If anything below is unclear, email us at theforgeapp@gmail.com.

At a glance

Forge requires an account so we can attribute foods you contribute to the shared database and prevent abuse. Sign in with Google or Apple. We receive your name and email from the provider — that's all.
Your food log, weight, profile, and goals stay on your device. We do not store them on our servers.
When you add a food that isn't in the database, you choose whether to share it with the public Forge food database (visible to all users) or keep it in your personal database (stays on your device).
Camera permission is requested so you can scan barcodes. Frames are never recorded or transmitted.
We do not sell your data. No advertising, no advertising trackers, no data brokers — ever. We do use one anonymous analytics tool (PostHog) for internal product improvement; details below.
You can delete your account and any contributions at any time.


Account information
You sign in with Google or Apple. We use OAuth; we never see or store your password.
What we receive:

Sign in with Google — your name, email address, and Google account ID.
Sign in with Apple — your name (you can edit what we see), an email address (or Apple's @privaterelay.appleid.com relay address if you choose Hide My Email), and an opaque Apple user ID.

What we use it for:

One account per user, so the shared food database stays trustworthy.
So you can manage your public food contributions across reinstalls and devices.
To send you occasional account-related messages (sign-in confirmations, deletion confirmations). We do not send marketing email.

Account information is stored on Forge's authentication infrastructure. It is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

What stays on your device
Your day-to-day data is local. We do not transmit it to our servers. This includes:

Food log entries (which foods, in what quantities, on which days, in which meals)
Weight history
Profile data (age, biological sex, weight, height, body-fat percentage if provided, activity level, goal, experience level)
Custom goal profiles and macro targets
Meal templates, favorites, and recently logged items
Foods you keep in your personal database (i.e., not shared to the public Forge database)

We do not back this up or synchronize it across devices in v1.

Foods you contribute (per-food choice)
When you add a food that isn't in the database via the Contribute Food flow, you make one decision: public or personal.
ChoiceWhat happensKeep in personal databaseThe food's data stays on your device. We never see it.Share with the public Forge databaseThe food's nutritional data (name, brand, serving size, macros, optional micros, optional barcode) is uploaded to our servers and added to the shared database visible to all Forge users. Your display name appears as the contributor only if you opt in to attribution.
You can change your mind. Any food you've shared with the public database can be unshared from Profile → Contributions at any time. Unsharing removes the food from the public database going forward; food-log entries other users have already saved are unaffected because those entries live on their devices.
What gets uploaded is the food's nutritional data — never your food log, your weight, or anything that ties the food to your personal eating pattern.

Camera
Scanning a product barcode requires camera permission. Frames are processed by your device's barcode-recognition framework to extract a numeric value. Frames are not saved to disk and are not transmitted. The recognized barcode is checked against the public Forge database (when you have a network connection) and the bundled fallback list; the scan itself is not stored.

Analytics
We use PostHog to collect anonymous, aggregate usage signals — things like which screens are opened, which features are used, and where the App crashes. This helps us prioritize bugs and decide what to build next. The data is anonymous and is used solely for internal product improvement; it is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data. Not now, not ever.
We do not share, rent, or trade your data with anyone.
We do not display advertising.
We do not use third-party SDKs for advertising, attribution, or fingerprinting.
We do not track you across other apps or websites.
We do not access your contacts, photo library, microphone, precise location, biometrics, or advertising identifiers.

Children
Forge is not directed to children under 13. The onboarding flow asks for age and rejects values below 13. If we learn that we have account information belonging to a child under 13, we will delete it.

Your rights and controls

Access. Your local data is viewable inside the App. Your account information (the name and email from your sign-in provider) is shown on your Profile screen.
Export. Not available in v1. CSV export is on the v2 roadmap.
Delete your local data. Profile → Reset & Restart Onboarding clears all profile, log, and personal-database data on the device.
Delete your account. Profile → Delete Account permanently deletes your Forge account and any foods you've shared with the public database. The action is immediate and irreversible. Your name and email are scrubbed from our systems promptly.
Unshare a contribution (without deleting your account). Profile → Contributions → Unshare removes a single food from the public database.

If you live in a jurisdiction with statutory privacy rights (for example, the EU's GDPR or California's CCPA), the controls above are the same controls those statutes guarantee. If you have questions about how a specific right applies to your account, email us.

Data retention
Account information is retained until you delete your account. Public food contributions are retained for the lifetime of the shared database unless you unshare them. Local device data is retained until you delete it (via in-app reset, account deletion, or by uninstalling the App, subject to OS-level backups your device may keep).

Changes to this policy
If we change this policy, the Effective date at the top will move forward and a note will appear in the App's release notes. Material changes will be highlighted in-app on the first launch following the update.
